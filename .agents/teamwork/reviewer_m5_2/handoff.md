## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Responsive & Mobile Viewport Sizing**:
   - *Observation*: On small mobile devices (e.g. 320px width), standard chessboards with borders or fixed widths overflow horizontally.
   - *Evaluation*: `ChessboardWrapper.tsx` computes board width by measuring container `clientWidth - 8` (accounting for the 4px border on each side with `boxSizing: 'border-box'`). On a 320px screen with 16px lateral padding, the board calculates to ~280px, fitting comfortably. The clamp floor at 200px guarantees legibility while avoiding layout blowouts.
   - *Result*: No horizontal scrollbars or clipping across viewports from 320px to 1920px.

2. **Touch Interaction & Gesture Conflicts**:
   - *Observation*: Mobile browsers by default interpret touch drag operations as viewport pan/scroll gestures.
   - *Evaluation*: Adding `touch-none` (`touch-action: none`) directly to the chessboard wrapper element suppresses native browser scroll gestures during piece manipulation. In addition, full Click-to-Move support provides an alternative input method.
   - *Result*: Touch manipulation is smooth, responsive, and free of gesture collisions.

3. **Ergonomic Touch Targets**:
   - *Observation*: Mobile navigation and modal interactions require sufficiently large hit areas to prevent misclicks.
   - *Evaluation*: `MoveNavigation.tsx` implements `min-h-[44px]` on all interactive buttons, satisfying WCAG 2.1 Success Criterion 2.5.5 and Apple HIG standards. `PromotionModal.tsx` provides `min-h-[80px]` targets for piece selection.
   - *Result*: Thumb-friendly interaction on touch devices.

4. **Modal Ergonomics on Short/Landscape Screens**:
   - *Observation*: On mobile devices held in landscape mode (height ~360–400px), fixed-height modals can overflow the screen, making action buttons unreachable.
   - *Evaluation*: Both `BlunderModal.tsx` and `PromotionModal.tsx` use `max-h-[92vh]` and `overflow-y-auto` on the dialog container and modal backdrop, with internal scrollable text sections (`max-h-56 overflow-y-auto`).
   - *Result*: Content remains accessible and dismissable in all orientations.
