## 2. Logic Chain

1. **Root Cause**:
   - In `colle-ohanlon-1930`, step 1 represents White's move `13.Ng5+` leaping from `f3` to `g5`.
   - The destination square `g5` lies on Rank 5.
   - The FEN notation in line 94 erroneously placed the knight on `g6` (Rank 6: `3bp1N1`), leaving Rank 5 empty (`8`).
   - Consequently, when `CalculationTrainer` executed step 1, line 440 (`setBoardFen(currentStep.expectedFenAfter)`) rendered an impossible board state with White's knight on `g6`.

2. **Challenger Assertion Failure**:
   - `tests/challenger_m4_empirical_test.ts` (test CH-M4-03) strictly validates that `expectedFenAfter` equals the board position generated by `chess.js` after 13.Ng5+.
   - Because `CalculationTrainer.tsx` line 94 has `3bp1N1/8` instead of `3bp3/6N1`, CH-M4-03 failed with an assertion error.

3. **Scope of Defect**:
   - `colle-ohanlon-1930` is the only study with a specialized hardcoded branch in `getCalculationStepsForStudy` (lines 68-165).

   - The platform-wide audit verified that all 93 dynamically generated steps have correct FENs and legal move transitions.
